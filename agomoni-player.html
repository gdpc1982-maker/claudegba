Agomoni: Mahalaya 2026 live radio, Durga Puja songs and countdown

Agomoni is a free website for the Durga Puja season. It plays the Mahalaya radio broadcast live, has playlists of Durga Puja and Mahalaya songs, a Dhak button for the sound of the drums, and live countdowns to Mahalaya and to Durga Pujo.

When is Mahalaya 2026?

Mahalaya 2026 is on Saturday, 10 October 2026. The traditional Mahishasuramardini radio programme, with its Chandipath recitation, starts early in the morning at about 4:00 AM IST (Indian Standard Time, Kolkata).

What time is Mahalaya 2026 in the USA, UK, Australia and the Gulf?

The broadcast starts at 4:00 AM IST on Saturday 10 October 2026. That is about 11:30 PM on Friday 9 October in London (BST), 6:30 PM on Friday in New York (EDT), 3:30 PM on Friday in Los Angeles (PDT), 2:30 AM on Saturday in Dubai, 4:30 AM in Dhaka, 6:30 AM in Singapore and 9:30 AM in Sydney (AEDT). Agomoni shows the time in your own time zone.

How can I listen to Mahalaya live online?

Open this page and press play on the live radio. The player streams Akashvani FM Rainbow Kolkata, which carries the Mahalaya broadcast. You can also tap the bell to get a reminder notification at 4:00 AM IST.

Durga Puja 2026 dates

  • Mahalaya: 10 October 2026
  • Shashthi: 16 October 2026
  • Saptami: 17 to 18 October 2026
  • Ashtami: 19 October 2026
  • Nabami: 20 October 2026
  • Bijaya Dashami: 21 October 2026

What can I listen to?

Besides the live radio, the Playlists button opens Mahalaya recordings, Mahalaya songs and Durga Puja songs. Songs play in full for everyone through YouTube, including the full Mahishasuramardini recording by Birendra Krishna Bhadra.

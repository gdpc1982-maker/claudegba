Everything You Need to Know About GBA & Durga Puja

About GBA

Gurgaon Bengalee Association (GBA) has been the home of the Bengali community in Gurgaon since 1982, bringing together over 1000 Bengali families through festivals, cultural programmes and community events throughout the year.

Yes — Gurgaon Bengalee Association, formally registered in 1996, is Gurugram's first statutory Bengali cultural organisation, dedicated to preserving Bengali language, culture and traditions in the city.

Durga Puja celebrations in Gurgaon include several community pujas, but GBA's Durga Puja at the HUDA Community Center, Sector 9, Gurugram stands out as the city's oldest — running continuously since 1982. Free bhog for everyone and open cultural programmes with no reserved seating for members make it one of the most welcoming and inclusive Durga Puja celebrations in the city.

You can join GBA by reaching out through the Contact Us page — membership, sponsorship and volunteering enquiries are all welcome.

Understanding Durga Puja

A major Hindu festival celebrating Goddess Durga's victory over the demon Mahishasura, symbolizing good triumphing over evil. Originating in Bengal, it's celebrated over five main days each autumn (Ashwin, September–October). In Gurgaon specifically, GBA has organised the city's oldest Durga Puja at the HUDA Community Center, Sector 9, since 1982.

The demon Mahishasura became invincible through a boon, so the gods combined their powers to create Durga, who battled him for nine days and nights before defeating him on the tenth — Vijaya Dashami.

Panchami (festivities begin) → Shashthi (goddess unveiled) → Saptami (Kola Bou ritual, bhog) → Ashtami (Sandhi Puja, the most significant moment) → Navami (gratitude rituals, grand aarti) → Dashami (Sindoor Khela, immersion).

It marks the exact junction between Ashtami and Navami — the moment Durga is believed to have taken her fiercest form to slay Mahishasura, considered the most powerful moment of the festival.

On Vijaya Dashami, married women apply vermillion to the goddess and each other as a farewell blessing before immersion.

FY 2025–26 Highlights

A quick look at what GBA achieved this past year.

  • Registered under the Government of India's NPO Darpan framework, strengthening GBA's legal and institutional standing.
  • Won 2nd Prize for Best Pratima at the JPC Bijoya Sammilani.
  • Inaugurated a brand-new GBA Club House, a permanent home for community activities.
  • Organised a Medical Camp with Manipal Hospital and a CPR Training Workshop with Max Hospital.
  • Donated toys, sarees and food packages to underprivileged families through community welfare drives.
  • Contributed to Calcutta flood relief via the Delhi Bengal Association.
  • Grew to 155 active members, with a new renewal cycle underway.
  • Set up a Sports & Recreation area (Table Tennis, Chess, Carrom) at the Club House.
  • Launched plans for a Dance School and Library at the Club House.

Plus 25 activities in all — from Durga Puja and Kali Puja to sports tournaments and statutory compliance milestones.

Durga Puja 2026 Schedule

Six days of rituals, culture and community — 15–21 October 2026 at HUDA Community Center, Sector 9, Gurugram.

Maha Panchami

15 October

The beginning of festivities as the community comes together to welcome the season.

Maha Shashthi

16 October

Welcome ceremony of Maa Durga with traditional rituals and cultural programmes.

Maha Saptami

17–18 October

Morning puja, bhog distribution and community participation.

Maha Ashtami

19 October

Pushpanjali, Sandhi Puja, celebrations and cultural performances.

Maha Navami

20 October

The final day of puja, marked by rituals of gratitude and community bhog.

Vijaya Dashami

21 October

Sindoor Khela, farewell rituals and immersion ceremony.

The pandal is open 24×7 to all visitors across every day of the festival — Maha Panchami to Vijaya Dashami.

Dates as per the Bengali Panchang and subject to final confirmation closer to the festival. Have a question? Check our FAQ →

Durga Puja 2026 Schedule

Six days of rituals, culture and community 15–21 October 2026 at HUDA Community Center, Sector 9, Gurugram.

Maha Panchami

Thursday, 15 October 2026

The beginning of festivities as the community comes together to welcome the season.

Evening

  • 7:00 PM onwardsAnanda Mela – Food FiestaA festival of joy & food. Good food, great company, happy moments.

Food Fiesta menu

  • Lucknowi Paratha + Kebab
  • Chowmein & Chicken Roll
  • Malpua
  • Ghugni & Patishapta / Naru
  • Bhapa Doi & Chocolate
  • Sweet Corn
  • Cold Coffee & Chicken Pakora

Maha Shashthi

Friday, 16 October 2026

Welcome ceremony of Maa Durga with traditional rituals and cultural programmes.

Morning puja

  • 8:00 AM–11:30 AMPuja start and end
  • 9:30 AM & 10:30 AMPushpanjaliApproximate timings

Thakurer Bhog Prasad

  • Panch Bhaja
  • Payesh (Kheer)
  • Luchi (Puri), Suji & Chatani

Evening

  • 6:30 PMBodhon and Sandhya Arati
  • 7:30–7:45 PMInauguration – lamp lighting, speech by President GBA
  • 7:45–8:30 PMAgomoni Gan – directed by Rupali Sinha
  • 8:30–8:40 PMPoem and song presentation – Prapti Samanta
  • 8:40–8:45 PMDance performance based on the Agomoni theme – Munmun, Soumi, Sarika
  • 8:45–9:45 PMBangla, Bollywood and beyond – Sanchari Mitra, Soumitra Ghosh (Guest), Debasish Bhattacharya

Pushpanjali timings are approximate and may change with the puja rituals.

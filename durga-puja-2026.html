Durgotsav 2026: Schedule & Photos

Six days of rituals, culture and community 15–21 October 2026 at HUDA Community Center, Sector 9, opposite Abhinav Apartment, Gurugram. Each day shows its programme with the photos from that morning and evening; tap any photo to see it full screen.

Bhog & Langar — free for everyone, served daily from 1:00 PM, Maha Saptami to Maha Navami (17–20 October). All are welcome.
The pandal is open 24×7 to all visitors, Maha Panchami to Vijaya Dashami.
Sandhi Puja · Monday 19 October · 10:28–11:16 AM

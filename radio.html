What Is Mahalaya?

Mahalaya marks the start of Devi Paksha and the countdown to Durga Puja. For generations, Bengali families have woken before dawn to hear Mahishasuramardini, the radio programme with its Chandipath recitation that begins at about 4:00 AM IST on Mahalaya morning. It is broadcast live from Kolkata on Akashvani FM Rainbow, and Durga Puja Radio brings it to you wherever you are.

Our Durga Puja

Gurgaon Bengalee Association organises Gurugram's oldest Durga Puja, a tradition that began in 1982 and has grown alongside the city itself. What started as a small gathering of Bengali families has become one of the most anticipated cultural celebrations in the region, bringing together devotion, music, theatre and community spirit over six days of festivities.

The pandal is open 24×7 to all visitors across every day of the festival — Maha Panchami to Vijaya Dashami.

Dates as per the Bengali Panchang and subject to final confirmation closer to the festival. Have a question? Check our FAQ →

Venue: HUDA Community Center, Sector 9, Gurugram

Good to Know

Anjali Timing

Pushpanjali is offered every morning from Shashthi to Navami, following each day's auspicious timing. See the day-wise schedule for exact times.

Bhog

Free bhog is served to everyone across all six days — no cost, no membership required. Serving times announced closer to the festival.

Parking is available for all visitors at the venue — no restrictions.
